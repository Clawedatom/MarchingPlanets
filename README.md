# MarchingPlanets
CheddarCat Virtual Studio — Game Prototype

## Overview
MarchingPlanets is a sci-fi exploration, survival, and settlement-building game set in an unknown solar system.

You are the leader of a group of alien survivors who have crash-landed on a hostile planet. With your settlement struggling to survive, you must take control of an advanced Mech suit and venture across the planets of the solar system in search of the resources needed to build a new home.

### Explore. Gather. Upgrade. Defend. Escape.
As you explore each planet, your Mech can be equipped with specialised attachments that allow you to gather valuable resources, discover new technologies, and uncover the secrets of the solar system. Bring your findings back to the settlement to unlock:

🏗️ New buildings and settlement facilities

👽 New NPCs and survivors

⚙️ Mech upgrades and new capabilities

🛡️ Defensive structures and technologies

🔬 Advanced technologies required for escape

However, your survival is about to become much more complicated.

### The Threat
Early in your journey, you discover evidence of an unknown entity attempting to assimilate the entire solar system.

The entity has begun isolating the system from the rest of the galaxy, preventing any possibility of escape. Worse still, it is sending increasingly powerful alien swarms to locate and overrun your settlement.

As your technology advances, so does the threat.

### Survive & Defend
Between expeditions, you must strengthen your settlement and prepare for increasingly dangerous attacks.

Expand your base, develop new technologies, upgrade your Mech, and construct defensive systems capable of surviving progressively stronger alien assaults.

Your Mech isn't just a tool for exploration — it is your settlement's most powerful line of defence.

### The Goal
Your ultimate objective is to advance your settlement's technology far enough to discover a way out of the solar system.

To achieve this, you will need to:

Explore the planets within the solar system.

Gather resources using your Mech and its specialised attachments.

Expand your settlement and unlock new buildings, NPCs, and technologies.

Upgrade your Mech with new tools, abilities, and combat capabilities.

Defend your settlement against increasingly powerful alien attacks.

Uncover the secrets behind the entity and its attempt to assimilate the system.

Escape the solar system before your settlement is overwhelmed.

MarchingPlanets combines planetary exploration, resource gathering, settlement progression, Mech customisation, and defensive combat into a single sci-fi survival experience.

Explore the unknown. Build your future. Defend your people. Find a way home.
